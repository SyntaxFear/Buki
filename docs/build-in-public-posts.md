# Buki platform copy guide

The weekly X manifest and legacy daily `session.md` files contain the exact drafts. These patterns keep the voice consistent.

## X

Plan X once each Saturday as a 14-post Sunday–Saturday batch. Follow `docs/build-in-public-weekly-x-runbook.md`. Before writing or editing any post, load `/Users/bitcoin/Developer/skills/plainspoken/SKILL.md`, its `references/voice-profile.md`, and its `references/approved-examples.md`.

- **Early post:** use a unique time from 13:00–13:30 `Asia/Tbilisi`.
- **Late post:** use a unique time from 18:30–19:00 `Asia/Tbilisi`.

Every post requires unique verified media. Keep each post under 280 characters and use 3–4 relevant hashtags including `#Shipaton #BuildInPublic`. Exactly ten weekly posts use a specific answerable question. Avoid hype, polished AI conclusions, generic engagement bait, unstable status claims, metrics, and unrelated trending tags.

## LinkedIn

Explain what was difficult, the decision made, and what feedback could change. Avoid launch language until public availability is verified.

## Discord

Keep it short:

> I’m building Buki, a private little book for children’s drawings. Today I’m testing [specific feature or decision]. [One sentence of context.] Feedback I’d value: [one question].

Use the official Shipaton `#post-engagement-boost` channel when appropriate. Do not label posts “Day 2” or imply the product was built in only a few days.

## Reddit

Lead with a useful question or technical lesson. Include enough context in the post itself. Confirm the community’s current self-promotion rules before posting and remove all decorative hashtags.

## Dev.to

Use source-backed technical articles for the extraction pipeline, privacy model, and archive/export design. Suggested tags are `shipaton`, `buildinpublic`, `reactnative`, and `expo` when genuinely relevant.
